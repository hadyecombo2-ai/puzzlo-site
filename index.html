    Why we make puzzles

    Small pieces, big quiet.

    Puzzlo began with a simple wish: to put down the phone and pick up something that rewards attention. Every picture we make starts from a place on the Gulf coast we love, then becomes a hundred, five hundred, a thousand small decisions.

    Curiosity
    A corner of sky, a coral door. You start anywhere and keep looking.
    Discovery
    That small click when a piece fits, or a ring finally slips free.
    Shared time
    One table, a pot of tea, and everyone pointing at the same edge.

    Give an evening, not just a gift.
